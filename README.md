# ResBot

Your resume bot.

## What it does
- **Analyze a resume against a job description** — assesses how well your resume matches a JD, provides an ATS-style score, and gives targeted suggestions for improvement.
- **Maintain a personal profile** — stores your full professional information so ResBot always knows what it's working with, and can refine it as you update things.
- **Generate tailored resumes** — creates a resume optimized for a specific job description, honoring constraints like fitting on a single page or keeping required sections.
- **Iteratively edit resumes** — chat with ResBot to refine your resume, seeing live-rendered PDF updates as changes are made. Constraints and previous decisions are preserved throughout the session.
